# Clip preferences

What makes a good clip for your channel. Claude reads this before picking moments and writing edit recipes,
and adds to it after each round of your feedback. Copy this file to `preferences.md` (which is git-ignored)
and replace the examples with your own.

## Channel
- Game(s): e.g. Valorant, PUBG. Solo / duo / squad.
- Spoken language on stream: e.g. Hindi mixed with English (or "auto").
- Caption language for the audience: e.g. English. Captions should carry the emotion, not translate literally.
- Facecam: yes / no. Live chat worth showing: yes / no.
- Output: e.g. 5-8 vertical Shorts per 2-hour stream, plus one horizontal montage.

## Call to action (optional)
- Channel URL: e.g. https://www.youtube.com/@yourchannel
- End-of-Short text: e.g. "Full stream on the channel 👆", shown for the last ~1.5 s. Keep it small and
  short; a Short that ends on a joke works better than one that ends on an ad.
- Description lines to add to every upload: e.g. a subscribe link (`<channel URL>?sub_confirmation=1`),
  "Edited with ClutchCut: <repo link>", the music credit.

## Squad (optional)
- Your in-game name and teammates' names, if you want them named in edits. Leave empty to keep everyone anonymous.

## Style
- What you want: good plays, team banter, rage / hype, funny deaths...
- What you don't want: plain uncut clips, hard cuts, heavy fonts...
- Music: only tracks you have the right to use (see docs/licenses.md).
